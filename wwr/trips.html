Our Rafting Trips

Half-Day Adventure

Class II–III • 4 Hours • $89/person

Perfect for beginners and families looking for an exciting introduction to white water rafting. You will paddle through scenic stretches of the river, tackle moderate rapids, and enjoy a riverside snack break. All equipment and a certified guide are included. No experience needed — just bring your sense of adventure!

Full-Day Canyon Run

Class III–IV • 8 Hours • $159/person

Our most popular trip takes you deep into the canyon for a full day of adrenaline-pumping rapids and breathtaking scenery. A riverside lunch is served on a secluded beach midway through the journey. This trip is ideal for those with some rafting experience who want to push their limits and explore hidden corners of the river.

Overnight Expedition

Class III–V • 2 Days • $349/person

The ultimate rafting experience. Spend two days on the river, camping under the stars at a pristine riverside campsite. You will navigate the most challenging rapids on our route, enjoy chef-prepared meals, and connect with nature in ways few ever experience. This trip includes all meals, camping gear, and transportation back to base.
