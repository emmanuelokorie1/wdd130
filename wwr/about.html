History

Rapids! started floating the river in 1995. What began as a single raft and a dream shared by two brothers has grown into one of the region's most trusted white water outfitters. We remain a family owned and operated business dedicated to safe, unforgettable river experiences.

Over three decades we have guided thousands of adventurers through Class II to Class V rapids. Our experienced guides know every turn of the river and are committed to helping first-timers and seasoned paddlers alike make lasting memories on the water.
